# Style bible — the 3D explainer-shorts genre (measured, not guessed)

Sources: public frames of the genre's biggest channel (Zack D. Films — studied as a reference, never copied):
400 most recent shorts (titles + view counts), frame 0 of the top 60 (the hook frame), and 5 frames each (first,
25 %, 50 %, 75 %, thumbnail) of 12 top videos about everyday things; plus a credited contractor's description of the
production pipeline (Blender + physics-assisted character animation + asset libraries + compositing, 100+ shorts).
To study any video frame-by-frame yourself: `s3d.py analyze video.mp4`.

## Numbers
- Median views across 400 shorts: 9.9 M. Title patterns (median): What If 15 M · Why 12 M · Can/Does 11 M ·
  Patent Pending 11 M · (fiction) 14 M · How X Works 7.3 M (but the top video, 78 M, is "How Electrical Outlets Work").
- Titles: 3–9 words (median 6.5) + exactly one emoji at the end: 🤔 93 · 😮 76 · 😨 41 · 🤨 27 · 😱 21 · 😢 19.
- Length 20–60 s (job briefs ask for 20–45 s); hook must land in 1–2 s; a cut every ~2–3 s.

## Hook frame (frame 0) — top 40
~50 % open on **hands acting on an everyday object in close-up, mid-action** (plugging a charger, cutting, holding,
pinching); the rest open on a character already in a relatable situation, a creature/object macro, or a POV
(steering wheel, handlebars). Narration and captions are already running at frame 0 — no intro, no title card.

## Visual language
- Semi-realistic humans (realistic skin & hair, slightly stylised proportions), big readable expressions.
- Brand everywhere, consistently: logo on t-shirts, product labels, phone screens, steering wheels; parody packaging
  instead of real brands.
- Palette: cyan/sky-blue dominant (seamless coloured cyc backdrops, blue walls), warm skin, saturated props.
- Lenses: wide-angle close-ups (object near the lens, food huge before a bite), macro inserts with shallow DOF,
  POV hands, top-down, low hero angles, foreground occluders (bars, doorframes) for depth.
- Explainer devices: translucent x-ray skin with organs; circular cutaway holes in body/shirt; anatomical
  cross-sections on flat teal; thought-bubble masks; "tiny workers inside the machine" metaphor with a giant face
  peeking in; glowing energy (arcs, red sound waves, pressure glow); 2D cyan arrows/chevrons; measurement callouts.
- Payoffs: a gross/painful/funny exaggerated consequence or a satisfying practical result.
- Captions: white, extra-bold rounded sans, soft dark shadow, sentence case, 2–4 words per card, centred at
  ~78–80 % of the height. Accent colour sparingly.
- Sets: minimal — a few props, gradient skies, simple rooms; reuse sets across videos.

## Originality rules (protect your channel)
Use your own character presets, your own brand kit and colours, your own voice or licensed TTS, parody/no real
brands. Learn the grammar (hooks, pacing, devices) — never copy characters, logos, scripts or exact shots.
