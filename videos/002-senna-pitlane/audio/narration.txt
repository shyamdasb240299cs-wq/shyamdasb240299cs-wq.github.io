The fastest lap of the entire race was set by driving straight through the pit lane. Donington, 1993. Rain, then sun, then rain again. Ayrton Senna dives into the pits for fresh tyres... then changes his mind. He never lifts. Back then, there was no pit lane speed limit. And look where Donington's pit entry was. Right before the final hairpin. Every other car had to brake hard and swing all the way around it. The pit lane cut inside it. Two hundred metres shorter. He blasts back out, and the clock reads one minute, eighteen point zero two nine. Fastest lap of the race... and still the lap record there today. A year later, pit lane speed limits arrived. So for one wet afternoon at Donington...
