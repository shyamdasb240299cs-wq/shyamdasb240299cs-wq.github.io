# 002 · Senna's Pit-Lane Fastest Lap: asset list

Put every download in one folder, `senna-assets/`, using the **Save as** names below.
Sketchfab and Freesound need a free account to download. On Sketchfab, pick **glTF / .glb** (or the original .blend if offered).

## Track facts (from the real survey data)
- The 1993 pit entry is in OpenStreetMap as **"Old GP pit entry"** (no longer used for racing). It leaves the
  Melbourne return straight about 190 m before Goddards, loops inside the hairpin (a tighter left turn) and joins the pit lane.
- **Goddards is a LEFT-hand hairpin** (+159° turn). Melbourne Hairpin and Redgate are right-handers.
- Track route from the split to the pit exit: **782 m**. Through the 1993 pit lane: **581 m**. That is **200 m shorter (−26 %)**.
- GP lap length measured from the map: 4,005 m (official 4,020 m, so 0.4 % off). The 1 m LIDAR terrain matches the map lines.
